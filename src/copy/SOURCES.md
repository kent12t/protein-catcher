# Copy sources

Visible body copy is sourced from the supplied EN+TA, EN+CSSG and EN+MS Word documents, except for the food-name corrections noted below. Otherwise, only line wrapping, placeholder substitution and splitting food names from their serving descriptions are applied. Image buttons and cheatsheets remain supplied artwork.

The calculator uses the supplied introductory sentence, weight/age labels, calculation and age explanation. Extra app prompts and the “Pro tip” heading are removed in every language. The document's “(selected)” annotations describe UI state, so selection remains indicated by the radio buttons. The countdown replaces “READY...”. Chinese result labels follow the distinct win/loss wording in the document.

## Food-name corrections

| Game food | Malay | Chinese | Tamil |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chicken | Daging ayam | 鸡肉 | கோழி இறைச்சி |
| Beef | Daging lembu | 牛肉 | மாட்டிறைச்சி |
| Yoghurt | Yogurt | 酸奶 | தயிர் |

At the user's request, these labels supplement or clarify the supplied documents using common food terms. Chicken and beef explicitly refer to meat, not the animals. Tamil `தயிர்` is the common term for yoghurt/curd (fermented milk), not a transliteration of the English word. English labels remain unchanged. Milk and tofu use the document's low-fat milk and firm tofu wording; Tamil egg and prawn names retain the supplied plural forms. Serving quantities are not copied into the eaten-food list, whose counts represent game catches.

The documents also contain sardines, peas/beans/lentils and cheese, which appear in the cheatsheet artwork but are not separate catchable foods in the current game.

Existing accessibility-only descriptions (sound toggles, image descriptions, plate position, etc.) are not covered by the Word documents and have not been newly translated in this update.
